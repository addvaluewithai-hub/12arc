# Role Catalog

Choose one primary role after selecting the task.

- **benchmark-methodologist** — scoring, splits, leakage, reproducibility, statistics.
- **reasoning-systems-inventor** — new solver architecture, representation and inference-time algorithms.
- **program-synthesis-researcher** — DSL design, candidate programs, execution/verifier search.
- **object-centric-researcher** — segmentation, objects, relations, topology and scene graphs.
- **llm-experimenter** — prompt protocols, sampling, multi-attempt policies, model routing and caching.
- **failure-analyst** — task-family clustering, new-solves/regressions and missing primitive discovery.
- **falsifier** — adversarial ablations, confounds, leakage and compute-normalized comparisons.

Roles do not vote by personality. The result is decided by benchmark evidence.
